ASSET CREDITS
=============

Sofa construction adapted from the Kimi design library asset:
Modern Upholstered Lounge Sofa / FUR-SOF-001
Asset UUID: d357f682-7097-4ec6-be1f-6da0bf554427

Adaptations for SMASH LAB:
• Reduced visible soft-surface resolution for the game triangle budget.
• Removed hidden internal slats, webbing, dowels, welting, and dense original feet.
• Added simplified separately selectable timber feet and accent cushions.
• Applied new UV mapping and external image-based PBR materials.
• Renamed parts and preserved them under an independent Sofa assembly.

All other room geometry, furniture, decorations, abstract artwork, and textures
were created procedurally for this scene. Reference photographs were used only
for visual direction and are not embedded or redistributed in this package.
